# Mobile controls and original handling

## Touch-control research

Reviewed before implementing the joystick:

- [Apple: Design advanced games for Apple platforms](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10085/): use broad movement input areas, keep frequent actions near the thumbs, consider which actions must work together, respect safe areas, avoid covering the center, and give controls visible pressed states. Menus should hide gameplay controls.
- [Apple: Game controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-controls): virtual controls are appropriate for continuous movement and multiple actions; place them where they are comfortable to reach.
- [Android: Make apps more accessible](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/apps): use touch targets of at least 48×48 dp. The browser implementation uses a minimum 48 CSS-pixel hit area rather than equating CSS pixels with native dp on every device.

Application: a translucent floating joystick occupies the lower left of the track view, with a broad activation area and an 18% dead zone. Up/down control throttle and brake/reverse; horizontal travel gradually adjusts steering. This preserves the original car-relative steering instead of snapping the car toward a screen direction. The right thumb operates fire, turbo and mines. Weapon buttons disappear in weapons-off races, and all driving controls disappear while paused, at results or while spectating. Pointer capture, per-pointer ownership, cancellation and pause/rotation cleanup prevent held inputs from sticking. Keyboard steering overrides the joystick while a steering key is held.

## Practical limits

The browser still uses circle-based car contact hulls and sampled wall collision, rather than the original sprite-pixel collision and corner-blocking state. Its separation and angular contact calculation adapt the original response to those hulls. Linear impulses use the original 0.7 coefficient projected onto the circle contact normal, preserving tangential movement; applying the original full-vector impulse without its blocked-axis rollback pushed cars off the racing line. Wall damage uses the same armor/capped-impact conversion, with a configurable impact factor and the port's bounce response. The authored AI includes recovery and a corner-speed cap; probes now clamp at map boundaries as in the original. Bullet hit geometry, muzzle positions/spread, mine activation/blast geometry, and special terrain effects remain approximations. These differences are explicit; correcting the confirmed unit/conversion errors does not establish complete engine parity.

## Regression checks

`pnpm exec vitest run tests/unit/handling-parity.test.ts` covers steering conversion, human steering independence, slip memory, single-bullet damage, sustained firing and ammunition, weapon start protection, and contact impulses/damage. The original-asset matrix checks every stock car across all 19 circuit variants in traffic. Browser tests exercise the joystick and action buttons using real multi-touch events, including release, cancellation, rotation, hiding unavailable controls, and viewport fitting.
